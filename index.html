📋 Overview

Welcome to WordCraft. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you play our game.

Short version: WordCraft collects a minimal set of data necessary to provide cloud save, leaderboards, and multiplayer features. We do not sell your data or track you across other apps. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.

📦 Information We Collect MINIMAL

WordCraft collects the following data to operate its cloud features:

  • User / Device ID — A random anonymous identifier generated on first launch, used to associate your progress with a server-side account.
  • Email address — Only collected when you voluntarily sign in with Google or Apple. Used solely to identify your account across devices.
  • Display name & avatar — Shown on leaderboards and in multiplayer duels.
  • Game progress — Completed levels, star ratings, scores, daily streaks, and inventory (coins, power-ups).
  • In-app purchase records — Transaction IDs from the Google Play / App Store used to verify purchases. We never receive your payment card details.
  • IP address & user agent — Logged briefly in security audit events to detect cheating. Not used for advertising or tracking.

All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. We host on Vercel and Neon PostgreSQL, both of which are SOC 2 compliant.

🚫 What We Do NOT Collect

  • Precise location or GPS data
  • Device contacts, camera, or microphone access
  • Browsing history or cross-app activity
  • Payment card numbers or financial information
  • Advertising IDs or third-party tracking identifiers

🔗 Third-Party Services

WordCraft integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In — Optional account linking. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple Sign-In — Optional account linking on iOS. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing / App Store — For in-app purchases. We only receive a transaction ID and product ID; no payment details are shared with us.
  • Google AdMob — Shown to players who have not purchased the Remove Ads upgrade. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell or rent your data to any third parties.

👶 Children's Privacy

WordCraft is committed to following the Google Play Families Policy. The game does not include chat features, user-generated content, or social networking. No personal information is knowingly collected from children without parental consent.

The optional Google / Apple Sign-In feature requires users to have their own account with those providers. Anonymous guest play (no sign-in required) is always available and collects only a randomly generated device ID.

🔐 App Permissions

WordCraft requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function:

  • Internet — Required for cloud save, leaderboards, multiplayer, and optional OAuth sign-in
  • Storage — To save your game progress locally on your device

No sensitive permissions (camera, microphone, location, contacts) are requested.

🛡️ Data Security

All data in transit is protected by HTTPS/TLS. Server infrastructure is hosted on Vercel and Neon PostgreSQL, both SOC 2 compliant providers. Your data is never sold to or shared with advertising networks.

For anonymous (guest) players, only a randomly generated device ID is stored server-side. No email or real name is ever associated with a guest account.

🗑️ Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your WordCraft account and all associated personal data at any time. Once deleted, your player profile, game progress, leaderboard entries, purchase records, and any linked OAuth identity will be permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

Option 1 — Delete directly in the app (instant):

  1. Open WordCraft and tap the ⚙️ Settings button on the home screen.
  2. Scroll to the Danger Zone section.
  3. Tap 🗑 Delete Account and confirm both prompts.
  4. Your account is immediately deleted from our servers and the app resets to a fresh state.

Option 2 — Email request (if you no longer have the app):

Send an email with the subject line "WordCraft Account Deletion Request" to the address below. Include your in-game Player ID or the email address associated with your Google / Apple account so we can locate your record. We will process the request within 30 days and confirm by reply.

📧 Request Account Deletion via Email

What gets deleted: Player profile, display name, avatar, game progress, daily streaks, leaderboard scores, friendship graph, in-app purchase audit records, and linked OAuth identities (Google / Apple). Local device data is cleared automatically when you use the in-app option.
