Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2025

📋 Overview

Welcome to WordCraft. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you play our game.

Short version: WordCraft is an offline-first word puzzle game. We do not collect personal information, sell your data, or track you across apps.

📦 Information We Collect MINIMAL

WordCraft stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Game progress (current level, completed levels)
  • In-game currency (coins) and purchased power-ups
  • Daily challenge completion status
  • App settings and preferences

This data is stored in your device's local storage and is never transmitted to our servers.

🚫 What We Do NOT Collect

  • Your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • Location data or GPS information
  • Device contacts, camera, or microphone access
  • Browsing history or cross-app activity
  • Financial or payment information
  • Advertising IDs or tracking identifiers

🔗 Third-Party Services

WordCraft does not currently integrate with third-party analytics, advertising networks, or social platforms. No data is shared with any third party.

If we add third-party services in the future (e.g., crash reporting), this policy will be updated and users will be notified.

👶 Children's Privacy

WordCraft does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The game contains no chat features, social networking, or user-generated content.

Since all data is stored locally and no personal information is collected, the game is safe for all ages.

🔐 App Permissions

WordCraft requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function:

  • Internet — Required for potential future online features and updates
  • Storage — To save your game progress locally on your device

No sensitive permissions (camera, microphone, location, contacts) are requested.

🛡️ Data Security

Because all game data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. You can clear all game data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings.

We use industry-standard security practices and do not maintain any servers that store personal user information.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
